📅 Weekly Content Batching Schedule

The sustainable creator rhythm that maintains consistency without burnout

Monday: Planning Day

  • • Review analytics from last week
  • • Choose 3-5 hook templates for the week
  • • Write scripts for each video (15 min each)
  • • Prepare props/screenshots needed

Tuesday: Filming Day

  • • Batch film 5-7 videos (30-60 min total)
  • • Use natural lighting or ring light
  • • Film in portrait mode (9:16 ratio)
  • • Do 2-3 takes per hook for options

Wednesday: Editing Day

  • • Use CapCut, InShot, or native editors
  • • Add captions (80% watch without sound)
  • • Include hook text in first 2 seconds
  • • Add trending audio if relevant

Thursday: Scheduling Day

  • • Schedule posts for optimal times
  • • Instagram: 11am, 2pm, 7pm
  • • TikTok: 7am, 12pm, 7pm
  • • YouTube Shorts: 2pm, 6pm

Friday: Engagement Day

  • • Reply to all comments within 1 hour
  • • DMs from 'AUTOMATE' keyword responses
  • • Share user-generated content to Stories
  • • Engage with 20 accounts in your niche

Weekend: Rest & Optimize

  • • Review which hooks performed best
  • • Note engagement rates by content type
  • • Plan next week's content based on data
  • • Engage casually with community
